SafeAir

Every safety report is tied to a physical air gun, an energy cost and a verified fix.

The story

Unsafe open pipe→One-tap report→Automatic action→Measured safety and energy gain

Try it

  • Scan: scan a gun, pick the issue, add a photo or a note, send.
  • Stations: every gun is green, amber or red. Open FL2-03 to see why it is red.
  • Actions: assign a fix, enter the after readings, verify it. The station turns green and the dashboard updates.
  • Dashboard: air use, cost, unsafe events, noise, open reports and how fast they close.

What is new here

Guarded safety nozzles already exist. What is new is the loop around them:

  • A QR code links each report to one physical air gun.
  • Pressure, noise and airflow are recorded for every station.
  • The cost of every unsafe gun or leak is shown automatically.
  • Near misses can be reported in two taps.
  • An issue only closes after before and after readings prove the fix.
  • Operators confirm the new nozzle still cleans well.

Trust on the shop floor

Reports are linked to equipment and work zones, not to individual employees, and are used for improvement, not punishment. Teams only see their own progress against last month. Nobody is ranked.

Where readings come from

In the pilot, pressure comes from a gauge on the regulator and noise from a handheld sound meter at each check, entered in the app. Airflow is from the nozzle maker's data until a clamp-on flow meter is fitted.

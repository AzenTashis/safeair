SafeAir

A QR code on every compressed air point. Scan it, tap twice, and the report goes to the right person. Every report shows what the problem costs in energy, rupees and CO2, so a safety report is also a sustainability report.

Azen Tashis · B.Tech Mechanical Engineering · IIT Patna

Try it

  • Tap Scan an air point, then pick what you saw and press Send. That is the whole report.
  • Open My reports and tap a report to move it along, as maintenance would.
  • Open Plant to see energy and CO2 saved from fixed items add up.

How the cost of a leak is worked out

Air lost = 0.61 × hole floworifice table at 100 psig
Power = 0.18 kW × cfm18 kW per 100 cfm, US DOE
kWh a year = power × 4,800 htwo shifts, assumed
CO2 = kWh × 0.71 kgIndia grid factor, CEA
A clear hiss, about a 1/16 inch hole: 6.5 × 0.61 ≈ 4.0 cfm → 0.71 kW → ~3,400 kWh, Rs 27,000 and 2.4 t CO2 a year, at Rs 8 per kWh.

Why this fits L'Oréal

L'Oréal's sites already run on 100% renewable power, and its energy plan starts with "use less". Compressed air is one of the least efficient utilities in a plant, so every kWh this app finds is green power freed for other use. CO2 is shown at the India grid rate so that the saving can be compared with any other plant.

Built for the shop floor

  • Big tiles that work with gloves. Hindi labels with one tap.
  • Can be sent without a name, so people report near misses.
  • Credits go to the line team, not the person, so nobody games it.
  • Three reports at one point start a task-risk review of that job.
  • After every nozzle swap the app asks "Still cleans well?" Yes or No. That is the employee feedback measure.
  • A paper tag goes on the point too, so the fix is visible on the floor.

Demo data throughout. Flow figures from the ENERGY STAR orifice table; 0.61 is the sharp-edged hole factor, so leak costs are on the low side. Hours and tariff to be measured in the Week 1–2 survey.
